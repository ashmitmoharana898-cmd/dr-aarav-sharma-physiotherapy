PATIENT ADVISORY

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an initial consultation? expand_more
You can book directly via the digital booking form on this site, call our South Delhi practice desk at +91 98110 24891, or send an enquiry via WhatsApp / email to care@draaravsharma.in.
What should I expect during my first 60-minute assessment? expand_more
Your initial session consists of a comprehensive clinical history, full kinetic chain and biomechanical evaluation, orthopedic testing, diagnosis explanation, and an immediate preliminary hands-on treatment alongside your personalised roadmap.
Do I require a doctor's referral prior to treatment? expand_more
No referral is required. Direct consultation is standard clinical practice in India. You may schedule an initial biomechanical assessment directly. If you plan to claim reimbursement through private health insurance or a corporate TPA, we provide comprehensive itemized clinical receipts and medical documentation.
How many treatment sessions will I typically need? expand_more
Acute conditions often resolve substantially within 3 to 5 targeted sessions. Chronic dysfunctions or post-operative rehabilitations adhere to structured multi-phase programmes of 6 to 12 weeks with periodic progress testing.
Do you offer virtual or telehealth consultations? expand_more
Yes. We offer secure HD video consultations for patients across India (PAN-India) as well as international clients seeking specialist biomechanical guidance, ergonomic triage, and structured remote rehabilitation protocols.
What should I wear or bring to my appointment? expand_more
Please wear comfortable activewear or loose athletic clothing that allows unobstructed assessment of the affected joint or spine. Bring any relevant MRI, X-ray, or surgical discharge reports.
